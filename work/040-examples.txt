| Command | Prints (lines joined by " / ") |
|---|---|

| `casim solve '(SIN(A)^2+COS(A)^2-1+B)×X=1' X RAD` | `SOLVED: X=1÷(B+SIN(A)^2+COS(A)^2-1); B+SIN(A)^2+COS(A)^2-1≠0` |
