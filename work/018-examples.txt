# scripts/doc-examples.py (make check): each row's command must print exactly its answer or status. Every row with a
# whole lo and a symbolic or whole hi is also judged by value by scripts/sum-values.py, term by term.
# - Rules 4 and 5: polynomials (Faulhaber, to degree 12; 8 at the calculator's 8 KB), polynomial times geometric (B
#   rational, EXP(±1), SQRT(2) from 2^(K÷2)), and telescoping through partfrac, whose polynomial part joins the groups.
#   A constant factor comes out before partfrac (A÷(K×(K+1))).
# - Rule 2: a symbolic lo with a polynomial; the empty sum (hi < lo) is 0. A rational term with a symbolic lo is
#   NO_CLOSED_FORM (its answer would divide by lo).
# - Rule 7: whole bounds at most 64 apart are summed term by term (Σ 1÷K to 10; a pole outside the shifts' reach,
#   -1 to -1, which a telescoped form would name), in the request's angle mode; past 64, NO_CLOSED_FORM.
# - Rules 5, 6 and 8: named limits; poles in the range (from lo up, for a symbolic hi: the safe side, chosen); to ∞,
#   ∞ or -∞ by the dominant group (the larger |B|, then the degree), UNDEFINED where the partial sums swing, UNDECIDED
#   on a tie or an unknown sign. K÷(K^3+3×K^2+2×K) is 1÷((K+1)×(K+2)) where partfrac cancels K, a hole at 0.
# - From the cold review (fixed): a number past 2^62 keeps its text in the text it was read from (a bound holding one
#   is BAD_ARG; partfrac's are refused); a ratio that is 1 but not written so; 0^(K+1); 1÷EXP(K) and 1÷ALOG(K) as
#   EXP(-K) and 10^(-K); a polynomial's partial sums grow a degree faster than a swing's (equal growth: UNDECIDED); a
#   leading coefficient that may be 0 is UNDECIDED before a swing is UNDEFINED; symbolic bounds a known whole number
#   apart are the empty sum below lo; an exponent past 384 bytes is NO_ROOM, not integrate's word.
# - Rule 4b (Michael, via abacus #8293 and #8320): a letter in the ratio is answered with its rows, CANCEL's: X≠ each
#   real root of B = 1 (k×X^m solved for X; any other B as B≠1); at ∞, ABS(X)<r, or > for X^(-K), or ABS(B)<1. Beside a
#   divergent group without a letter, that group's ∞ with the letter's row; beside a swing, UNDECIDED. Whole bounds at
#   most 64 apart are summed term by term first (X+X^2+X^3, no row); past 191 bytes, the closed form and its row.
#   From rule 4b's cold review: X≠0 where the answer itself has no value at 0 though the sum may; a pole in the range
#   stays UNDEFINED beside a letter group. After abacus #8349: a letter group whose coefficient holds a negative power
#   of the ratio's letter is summed shifted (K×X^(K-1) as (K+1)×X^K from 0 to N-1), so its answer divides by no power
#   of X and needs no X≠0 the sum doesn't (K×X^(K-1), X^(2×K-1)). K×X^(K-2) keeps it: its first term is 1÷X. The
#   shift takes a ratio in one letter; Y^K×X^(K-1) (ratio X×Y) still shows X≠0.
# - After abacus's h018 (#8460): a telescoping sum typed as one n÷d is several terms once canon expands it, one per
#   power of K in the numerator, each over the one divisor. They are now one rational term, through partfrac once
#   (before, 7 terms passed the 6 allowed, and 4 terms of 3 fractions passed the 12 allowed). Fractions with the same
#   shift and power from different divisors are one entry too. The pole of S1581's divisor at K = 0 is in the range:
#   UNDEFINED.

#   separate fractions lost room (S0421 needed 9,260 B, NO_ROOM at 8,800). The room is given back after each
#   comparison, and these need what they did before the merge (8,540 B; 8,155 B).
