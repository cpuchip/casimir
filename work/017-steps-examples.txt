# CAS 017 steps' visible examples (work/017-substitution-and-parts.md §Steps, S4), written by casim from the build
# and read again by scripts/doc-examples.py (make check): each row's command must print exactly its walk, its pages
# joined by " / " with their indentation. Every walk here is also judged by scripts/istep-values.py (each line by value,
# BY PARTS's by (u×v)' - w with its labels: f = U×dv, V' = dv; the LN forms of S2.6).
# - SUBSTITUTION's U is printed as the answer's LN has it, in whole coefficients (U=5×X^3+2, its G rescaled);
#   a piece's walk is integrate's own on its text, in U.
# - BY PARTS's labels are the first two of U, V, W, T, S, R, Q free in the integrand (V and W inside a piece in U).
# - The polynomial loop is parts again; X^5×EXP(X)'s fourth level is one line, its answer (S2.3: three levels).
# - At 2,000 bytes X×EXP(X)'s walk doesn't fit: rule 8's one page (S2.4).
# - From the cold review: LN(X^2+1) walks by parts (a refused substitution's note once pointed into freed arena);
#   the cycle's 1-k bracketed (÷(10÷9)); a sum's negative term after its "+"; labels past U..Q. And where integrate
#   refuses (the letter rule: SIN(A×X)×COS(X)), the steps refuse with its status, not a walk of the parts. With
#   coefficients past 10^6 no whole form is made, and the LN keeps its ABS (an overflow once dropped it: a wrong
#   answer). Round 2: a substitution the plan can't replay in the room left (COS(X)÷(SIN(X)+2) at 5,000 B) is one
#   page, not parts the search never made.
# - At a page cap (abacus #8227, cap 192 as for integrate): a walk with a line past the cap is its one page, where that
#   fits (8 KB's LN(X+1) once refused: the count borrowed 384 bytes integrate needed). With --one-line the templates
#   aren't shown, and the walk fits. Where the one page itself is past the cap (197 bytes here), it is TOO_LONG, never
#   half a walk. A piece's walk is measured at the cap too: at 100 bytes ∫(2×X×SIN(3×X)÷3)dX is its one page inside
#   X^2×COS(3×X)'s walk. A sum's answer line is its own: LN(X)+ATAN(X) at 60 bytes is TOO_LONG, one page.
#   A walk that isn't 017's (CAS 013's, the table's) isn't measured, inside a 017 walk as at the top: at 80 bytes
#   ATAN(X+1)'s piece reaches a 98-byte SPLIT line and the walk stops TOO_LONG there with its whole count, as
#   X÷(X^2+2×X+2)'s own walk is TOO_LONG at 80. None of the examples does this from 100 bytes up.
