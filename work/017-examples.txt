# CAS unit 017 visible examples (work/017-substitution-and-parts.md), written by casim. Each row is a command and what it
# prints; scripts/doc-examples.py runs every row (make check), scripts/golden.py records each in the golden corpus, and
# scripts/integrate-values.py differentiates each answer numerically against its integrand.
# - Rule 3, substitution: u from a function, its argument or a power's base; G(U) by the whole procedure; X^3×SQRT(X^2+1)
#   by back-substitution (X^2 = U-1). DEG and GRAD carry their factor through u'.
# - Rule 4, parts by LIATE: the general step (LN(X), ATAN(X), LN(X)^2), the polynomial loop (X^5×EXP(X)), the cycle
#   (EXP(X)×SIN(X), in DEG with 1+π^2÷32400). ATAN(X) at 8 KB is by substitution (LN with ABS); at 64 KB its ∫v×u'
#   goes through the rational path (no ABS: X^2+1 is positive). Both are right.
# - Rule 2: X^2÷X×EXP(X), SIN(X÷X×X) and X÷(1000÷(SINH(π)×X)) lose a typed hole in canon: refused. INV(X^2)+3÷X+
#   0.5×X÷SQRT(X) keeps 0 out (its 3÷X): answered. EXP(A×X×Y) in Y would divide by A×X: refused; A×X×EXP(X^2) answers.
# - Rule 6: X-free summands dropped (X^2×ATAN(X)'s -1÷6); NO_ROOM for a sum's term the rational path ran out on at
#   8 KB; UNDEFINED for a pole; TOO_LONG past 191 bytes.
# - After the cold review: SGN, ABS and the opaque names (IP) holding X are refused (derive takes their derivative as
#   0), as is a literal canon keeps as typed (1.2.0); LN(ABS(X)) answers. The domain rule refuses LN(X)-LN(X)+X,
#   EXP(LN(X)), TAN(X)^0 and X^(-A)×X^A, whose canonical form drops a domain, and 0^X×0^(-X) (canon folds it to 1).
#   The letter rule refuses SIN(A×X)×COS(X) (÷(A^2-1)) and X×EXP(A×X^2)÷B (÷A), and keeps EXP(X)×SIN(A×X) (÷(A^2+1),
#   never 0).
# - Rule 5's depth: LN(X)^4 takes all four levels of parts and answers; LN(X)^5 is past them. At 2,000 and 1,500 bytes
#   a try that runs out of room is a refusal and the next answers (EXP(X)÷(1+EXP(X)), X^2×EXP(X)).
# - Rule 7's limits: trig powers, the ASIN form, and X×ATAN(X) at 8 KB (partfrac's 7,328 B can't nest).
# - Rule 8: steps give one page, the answer, for what only this path answers.
