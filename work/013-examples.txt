# - J0001 to J0004 are Thompson's XIX 9, 10, 12 and 13 (1914; problem text from pd-books' export 265b2e4).
#   XIX 9:  (5x+1)÷((x+2)(x-1)) = 3÷(x+2) + 2÷(x-1).
#   XIX 10: (x²-3)÷((x+3)(x-1)(x-2)) = (3÷10)÷(x+3) + (1÷2)÷(x-1) + (1÷5)÷(x-2), by cover-up at -3, 1 and 2.
#   XIX 12: 4x÷(x⁴-1) = 1÷(x+1) + 1÷(x-1) - 2x÷(x²+1).
#   XIX 13: 1÷(1-x⁴) = (1÷4)÷(x+1) - (1÷4)÷(x-1) + (1÷2)÷(x²+1).
# - LN of a linear factor has ABS; LN of a quadratic with D < 0 has none (rule 4). ATAN carries π÷180 in DEG and π÷200
#   in GRAD (J0012, J0013).
# - J0011: the common factor X-1 cancels, so it is refused (rule 8, #6708): the reduced function's antiderivative,
#   X-LN(ABS(X+2)), would be defined at X = 1 where the integrand isn't (CAS 006's guarantee: X^2÷X stays refused).
# - J0014 to J0017 are refused (rule 5, and rule 2 as ruled at #6686: integrate keeps NOT_INTEGRABLE for another
#   letter). J0018 is NO_ROOM at the 8 KB arena: partfrac needs 8,216 B for ten linear factors.
